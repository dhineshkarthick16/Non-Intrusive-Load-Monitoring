Only mains-contact point: the Rzc/D1/opto zero-cross tap is the single place this circuit touches live mains directly. The CT is clamp-on (no contact) and the opto provides galvanic isolation beyond that point — everything to the right of U1's dashed isolation barrier is safe low-voltage (3.3V) territory.
Assembly safety: wire the mains-side connections (CT clamp position, Rzc tap points) with the extension strip unplugged. Double-check D1's orientation before power-up — reversed, it won't protect the opto LED on the negative half-cycle.
